01 / The idea

What is Asparanza?

A marketplace where ability is seen, work is valued, and opportunity has room to grow.

Asparanza is an annual initiative organised by the Commerce Department of MES Mampad College. It gives makers with disabilities a place on campus to display and sell the products they create. The college provides stall spaces and essential support, while student coordinators work alongside the makers to help run each stall and connect their products with visitors.

The programme begins with an inauguration by invited guests and a moment of recognition for participating makers. Then the campus turns into a marketplace: products, conversations and small businesses meeting a wider audience. The proceeds from the makers’ product sales go to the makers themselves.

The programme is designed around practical help, shared responsibility and a direct connection between makers and buyers.

Students beside makers

06 / The institution

Rooted in Mampad.
Open to the future.

MES Mampad College is an autonomous institution in Mampad, Malappuram, Kerala. Established in 1965, the college has grown into a multidisciplinary centre for higher education. Asparanza is one way its Commerce Department connects learning with the wider community.
